Q42. Which molecule has the smallest bond angle?

sample

Answer: \( H_2O \)

  1. All three examples may involve \( sp^3 \) electron geometry.
  2. Lone pair–lone pair repulsion is stronger than bond pair–bond pair repulsion.
  3. Water has two lone pairs, so its bond angle is compressed most.

VSEPR theory: lone pairs occupy more space and reduce the visible bond angle.

Do not compare only hybridisation. Count lone pairs before deciding bond angle.
